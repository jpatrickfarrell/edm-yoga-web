The philosophy

Yoga was never just the poses.

Asana — the physical practice — is one limb of eight. EDM is built around all of them: how we breathe, what we believe, where we focus, how we live. That's true whether you're on a mat at 6am or in the middle of a festival crowd at midnight.

Everything in this ecosystem — the home studio, the certifications, the club sets — is one philosophy translated into a different room. The through-line is always breath.
