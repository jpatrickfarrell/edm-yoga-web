The Invitation

Come Home to Your Highest Self.

This weekend is an opportunity to get clear in your brand identity, to show up as the highest version of yourself, to teach from your heart, and to always remain both a student and a teacher of yoga.

Because yoga literally means unity, this is a community built on divine, unconditional love — the Embodied Divine Miraculous consciousness this whole practice is named for. We're here to connect to ourselves and to each other, to blend our humanness with our soul's perspective, to stay in alignment and take aligned action, and to manifest everything we need and more for the highest good of all. A space and a place for all of us to come together and heal as one unconditionally loving field of unity, peace, and togetherness.

What to pack Saturday morning

Wear your yoga fit, and pack one bag for the full day:

  • Bathing suit
  • A change of clothes, if desired
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat
  • Water
  • A very lightweight yoga mat, blanket, or Sand Cloud Towel
  • Anything else that helps you feel comfortable at the beach

Christina highly recommends a Sand Cloud Towel — it doubles as a meditation seat, beach blanket, and swim towel. Your mat and props can stay at Christina's home or in the Azzurra Yoga Room all weekend, so you won't need to carry them to the beach.

What to bring for the pajama party

Your coziest pajamas, and a printed photo of your inner child — a childhood photo of yourself, printed at 4x6 size. This goes in the middle of your frame, so don't forget it!
