The Five Kleshas

Find Out Which Tab Is Open.

Avidyā means incorrect comprehension — the moment we forget who we are. On your call, we'll find exactly which branch is running underneath your pattern, and open the door to its opposite.

Root Glitch Tab

Avidyā — Misperception

Forgetting your true nature, feeling separate from Source, the quiet sense that something is wrong with you. Closes with presence, breath, and remembering your connection to Source.

Survival Tab

Abhiniveśa — Fear of Loss

Fear of change, clinging to the known, hypervigilance and control patterns. Closes with deep safety, slow breath, and trust-building with the body.

Identity Tab

Asmitā — Ego-Identification

Over-identifying with roles, labels, wounds — "this is just who I am." Closes with witness consciousness: I am aware of this, therefore I am not only this.

Craving Tab

Rāga — Attachment

Chasing outcomes, needing things to feel okay, conditional happiness. Closes with gratitude, enoughness, and returning to now.

Avoidance Tab

Dveṣa — Aversion

Resisting discomfort, pushing away emotions, bracing against certain experiences. Closes with gentle feeling and allowing emotions safely.
