The Everything But Asana™ Method

Three Layers of Change.

Rooted in the limbs of yoga beyond the physical practice, expanded into a modern framework for personal transformation.

01 — Witness

Become the Observer

Help clients become the observer of their thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and patterns — witnessing what's happening with loving awareness instead of unconsciously reacting. This is where change begins.

02 — Release

Question the Pattern

Once a pattern can be witnessed, it can be questioned. Tools for examining limiting beliefs, conditioned responses, outdated identities, coping mechanisms, and stories that no longer serve the life your client wants.

03 — Realign

Consciously Choose What's Next

New thoughts. New behaviors. New standards. New boundaries. New habits. The goal isn't perfection — it's conscious participation in the creation of their life.

A Multi-Dimensional Framework

The 3D · 4D · 5D Framework.

Everything But Asana™ teaches you to look at transformation through multiple dimensions of experience.

3D — The Body

Physical Reality

Habits. Environment. Behavior. Embodiment. Daily choices. What is actually happening in your client's life?

4D — Breath & Nervous System

The Space Between

The space between stimulus and response. Breath becomes a bridge back into presence, so your client can respond consciously instead of automatically reacting from an old pattern.

5D — Mind & Possibility

Belief & Becoming

Beliefs. Identity. Vision. Meaning. Intention. Working with manifestation as a practice of becoming conscious of what we repeatedly think, believe, embody, and move toward.
